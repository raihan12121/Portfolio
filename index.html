Completed Thesis

Deepfake Image Detection — Cross-Dataset Generalization Study

Investigated deepfake detection generalization failures across diverse generative domains. Developed a dual-stream spatial and Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) frequency fusion network utilizing an EfficientNet-B4 backbone to identify imperceptible frequency-domain manipulation artifacts.

  • Dual-Stream Architecture: Fuses RGB spatial pixel representations with 2D FFT magnitude spectrums to expose high-frequency blending boundaries.
  • Cross-Dataset Benchmark: Rigorously benchmarked on FaceForensics++ (FF++), Celeb-DF, and DFDC datasets to quantify out-of-distribution domain shift.
  • Training & Robustness: GPU-accelerated training pipeline featuring targeted spatial augmentations, Gaussian blurring, compression artifacts, and adversarial perturbation testing.
Core Stack: PyTorch EfficientNet-B4 FFT Frequency Analysis OpenCV Weights & Biases
Time-Series ML Environmental AI
Research Study

Air Quality Index (AQI) Prediction — LSTM/BiLSTM vs. Classical ML Baselines

A comprehensive comparative study on multi-step air pollution and PM2.5 forecasting over multivariate hourly pollutant and meteorological time-series datasets.

  • Model Benchmarks: Implemented and evaluated BiLSTM, standard LSTM, statistical ARIMA, Random Forest, and XGBoost.
  • Empirical Outcome: Bidirectional LSTM (BiLSTM) captured forward and backward temporal dependencies with superior RMSE and MAE across extended multi-hour forecasting horizons.
Core Stack: Python PyTorch / Keras BiLSTM XGBoost Pandas / Scikit-learn
