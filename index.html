Undergraduate Thesis

Deepfake Image Detection — Cross-Dataset Generalization Study

Thesis Completed

Developed a dual-stream EfficientNet-B4 model fusing spatial and FFT-frequency features for deepfake detection. Evaluated on FF++, Celeb-DF, and DFDC datasets; studied cross-dataset generalization, frequency blending artifacts, and domain shift. Implemented a GPU-accelerated training pipeline with data augmentation and adversarial robustness testing.

  • Dual-Stream Architecture: Fuses RGB spatial representations with 2D FFT frequency magnitude features to expose boundary artifacts.
  • Datasets Evaluated: Rigorously benchmarked across FaceForensics++ (FF++), Celeb-DF, and DFDC datasets.
  • Robustness Pipeline: Built an end-to-end GPU training pipeline with targeted augmentations and adversarial robustness evaluation.
PyTorch EfficientNet-B4 FFT-based Forensics OpenCV Weights & Biases
